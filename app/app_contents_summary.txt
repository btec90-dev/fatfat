5. MATERNAL VITALS & CLINICAL DIAGNOSTIC ENGINE (ACOG & CDC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Blood Pressure Thresholds (ACOG):
  * Optimal: < 120 / < 80 mmHg
  * Elevated: 120-129 / < 80 mmHg
  * Stage 1 HTN: 130-139 / 80-89 mmHg
  * Gestational HTN Stage 2: 140-159 / 90-109 mmHg
  * Critical Alert (Severe HTN / Preeclampsia): >= 160 / >= 110 mmHg (Immediate maternal care required)
- Blood Glucose Targets (ACOG & ADA):
  * Fasting: <= 95 mg/dL
  * 1h Post-Prandial: <= 140 mg/dL
  * 2h Post-Prandial: <= 120 mg/dL
  * Hypoglycemia: < 60 mg/dL (Consume 15g fast-acting carbs)
- Weight Gain Tracking:
  * Rapid fluid retention warning: > 1.5 kg gain in < 7 days during T2/T3.

6. PREGNANCY GUIDE (MEDICAL ADVICE & HYGIENE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Prohibited Foods: Raw meats/fish, unpasteurized dairy, raw eggs, cured charcuterie (toxoplasmosis and listeriosis prevention).
- Hygiene: Washing raw fruits and vegetables with vinegared water.
- Supplements: Folic acid (Vitamin B9) indispensable in pre-conception and T1, Vitamin D3, iron, and iodine.
- Hydration: 1.5 to 2 liters of water daily. Zero alcohol. Limit caffeine to <200mg/day.
- Emergency Red Flags: Vaginal bleeding, contractions before 37 SA, fluid leakage, severe headaches with visual disturbances, sudden edema, decreased fetal movement, fever > 38°C.
